Could a 2-4 team BE any more optimistic? Two weeks ago, the Texans were 0-4. This past Sunday in London, they knocked off the division-leading Jaguars with a [walk-off field goal to cap a 34-31 shootout](https://www.fox26houston.com/sports/texans-jaguars-london-game). And it wasn't the vaunted Texans defense doing the heavy lifting at Wembley. For once, it was the offense, which has gone from a unit held together by the Nico Collins-CJ Stroud connection to one where Collins catches three balls and they still hang 34. Stroud is playing his ass off with [9 TDs, one pick](https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/S/StroCJ00.htm) and zero mid QB allegations (FAT paycheck incoming). Are the playoffs back in the picture, or is this a flash in the pan before an inevitable fade-out?

### WR Corps, Weakness to Strength

Three weeks into the season, the Texans' passing game was a cry for help. [Jayden Higgins went down in camp](https://www.nfl.com/news/texans-wr-jayden-higgins-torn-acl-out-2026-season), [Nico Collins followed with a hamstring injury](https://fieldlevelmedia.com/news/texans-star-wr-nico-collins-hamstring-to-miss-second-straight-game/), and CJ Stroud was left throwing to Xavier Hutchinson, Foster Moreau, and Jared Wayne. Meanwhile, Jaylin Noel, the most explosive receiver left on the roster, sat on the shelf (thanks, Nick Caley). The result was [18 points a game, 26th in the league](https://www.bleachernation.com/?p=2804819).

Fast forward to London. Stroud threw to [10 different pass catchers](https://www.espn.com/nfl/boxscore/_/gameId/401872996). Noel caught all four of his targets for [107 yards and a touchdown, his second straight game finding the end zone](https://www.thefantasyfootballers.com/news/643152/jaylin-noel-records-100-plus-yards-td-in-week-6/). Collins had three catches, and it didn't matter. Even Moreau, last month's emergency option, [caught two touchdowns](https://www.fox26houston.com/sports/texans-jaguars-london-game). And Marvin Harrison Jr., acquired from Arizona days earlier, [chipped in three catches in his debut](https://bleacherreport.com/articles/25512699-travis-hunter-shines-marvin-harrison-jr-debuts-box-score-stats-highlights-texans-vs-jags). Everyone eats.

For the non-believers, the Texans are finally using two top-ten YPRR (yards per route run) receivers in [Jaylin Noel (2.51)](https://sumersports.com/players/wide-receiver/?plays=100) and [Nico Collins (2.50)](https://sumersports.com/players/wide-receiver/?plays=100), min. 100 routes. Add a former top-five pick in MHJ, who [saw four targets in his debut](https://bleacherreport.com/articles/25512699-travis-hunter-shines-marvin-harrison-jr-debuts-box-score-stats-highlights-texans-vs-jags) after getting [just 12 in four games in Arizona](https://www.fox7austin.com/news/houston-texans-acquire-wr-marvin-harrison-jr-from-cardinals-major-trade.amp). Through six weeks, they're an above-average offense for the first time since 2023, with an explosive play rate of 10.9% ([sixth in the NFL](https://nflsavant.com/teams/HOU?tab=offense&year=2026)) and 0.02 EPA per play ([11th](https://sumersports.com/teams/offensive/)). That's a threat from anywhere on the field.

So who's fueling that offensive improvement? The one and only CJ Stroud. Last year, he had the EPA/play of a top-10 QB in the regular season ([0.09 EPA/play, 10th in the NFL](https://sumersports.com/players/quarterback/?plays=100&season=2025)), then fell on his face when it mattered most. Against the Patriots in the Divisional Round, as Texans fans (and everyone else, for that matter) will remember, he went [20 of 47 with four interceptions](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/47656592/cj-stroud-shoulders-blame-4-ints-texans-loss-pats), all in the first half, and Houston lost 28-16. Any criticism was rightfully deserved. The Texans reportedly offered around [\$50 million a year anyway](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/c-j-stroud-winning-rejecting-152426766.html), which would've slotted him [14th among QBs](https://www.spotrac.com/nfl/contracts/_/sort/average/dir/desc/position/qb). Stroud turned it down and bet on himself.

So far, the bet's paying off. Stroud opened the season with [216 straight attempts without an interception, a franchise record](https://www.houstontexans.com/news/cj-stroud-franchise-record-london-texans-jaguars-week-6). When the first one finally came, it was in the end zone at Wembley, with Houston trying to put the Jaguars away. [Travis Hunter picked it off, and Jacksonville kicked a 60-yarder to tie it](https://www.fox26houston.com/sports/texans-jaguars-london-game). Last January's Stroud probably throws another one and hands Jacksonville the game. Instead, he marched right back down the field and set up Fairbairn's walk-off. That poise and a [9-to-1 TD-to-INT ratio](https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/S/StroCJ00.htm) have him back in the top-QB conversation.

Want some advanced stats to back it up? He's up to [286](https://www.pro-football-reference.com/years/2026/passing.htm) passing yards a game from [217](https://www.pro-football-reference.com/years/2025/passing.htm) last year, 9th in EPA per play ([0.13](https://sumersports.com/players/quarterback/?plays=100)), and fifth in success rate ([49.4%](https://sumersports.com/players/quarterback/?plays=100)). That's being powered by above-average accuracy numbers. His [+3.9%](https://www.nfeloapp.com/qb-rankings/) CPOE ranks 12th, and his [bad-throw rate (13.9%) and on-target rate (76.7%)](https://www.pro-football-reference.com/years/2026/passing_advanced.htm) are both better than league average.

Improved CJ, plus a line that's actually protecting him? Baby, we got a stew going.

### $O-line: Less Worse?

Maybe it's more a Texas-style chili (no beans but all BEEF), but there's no denying the Texans O-line has stepped up over the past three games.

Through the first three games, the line gave up [10 sacks](https://www.statmuse.com/nfl/ask/texans-sacks-given-up-game-by-game). In the last three? [Four](https://www.statmuse.com/nfl/ask/texans-sacks-given-up-game-by-game) (with an assist from a Wembley turf that had pass rushers slipping all afternoon). Same goes for pressure rates. Stroud was pressured on [21.4%](https://www.pro-football-reference.com/years/2025/passing_advanced.htm) of his dropbacks in 2025, and this year it's down to [18.6%](https://www.pro-football-reference.com/years/2026/passing_advanced.htm#passing_advanced::30). That's while he's holding the ball longer ([2.77 seconds](https://sumersports.com/players/quarterback/?plays=100), up from [2.68](https://sumersports.com/players/quarterback/?plays=100&season=2025)). Despite pressure getting converted to sacks at a high rate ([14 sacks on 45 pressures](https://www.pro-football-reference.com/years/2026/passing_advanced.htm)), the O-line is giving CJ the time he needs to play with all his toys.

The run game, though? It's gone from bad to slightly less bad. Houston's averaging [84.8 rushing yards a game](https://www.pro-football-reference.com/teams/htx/2026/gamelog), up only a hair from before the winning streak, at 3.6 yards a carry. London, on the other hand? [114 yards at 4.6 a carry against the league's top-ranked run defense](https://www.click2houston.com/sports/2026/10/19/texans-make-a-statement-in-big-win-over-jaguars-at-wembley-stadium-in-london/), [without offseason signing David Montgomery](https://www.tsn.ca/nfl/article/texans-rb-montgomery-s-pitre-out-for-london-affair-vs-jaguars-n1-50192302/) [or Braden Smith](https://www.footballnationusa.com/post/texans-week-6-inactives-braden-smith-out-marvin-harrison-jr-active). Maybe we can throw some credit toward Nick Caley for (finally) adjusting his protection and run-blocking scheme. Houston lined up with an [extra offensive lineman on 35.6% of plays in London](https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/article/fantasy-football-texans-going-all-in-on-the-pass-game--and-its-revealed-a-new-flex-option-021011676.html), a season high. [Add Braden Smith coming off IR](https://clutchpoints.com/nfl/houston-texans/texans-news-houston-roster-move-gives-c-j-stroud-more-help-to-try-to-save-season), and the Texans have something going. Sometimes the fix is just more beef (and with Smith, hopefully better beef).

### Are the Texans Back?

Starting 0-4 is typically a death sentence. [Only one team has ever made the playoffs after it (the 1992 Chargers)](https://www.nbcsports.com/nfl/profootballtalk/rumor-mill/news/five-teams-face-an-0-4-start-and-only-one-0-4-team-has-ever-made-the-playoffs), and a month ago, I wasn't convinced the new-look Texans could be the second. But look at the next four games against the Giants ([starting their backup's backup, J.J. McCarthy](https://www.giants.com/news/j-j-mccarthy-to-start-quarterback-new-york-giants-week-7-houston-texans-john-harbaugh-jameis-winston)), the Chargers ([winless, with their OC on the hot seat](https://www.nbcsports.com/nfl/profootballtalk/rumor-mill/news/jim-harbaugh-mike-mcdaniels-job-is-not-in-jeopardy)), the Browns (come on, they're the Browns), and the Colts ([seemingly punting on the season after firing their GM](https://www.ksl.com/article/51638163/colts-fire-gm-chris-ballard-after-loss-to-titans)). My prediction of 3-5 entering Week 10 has been turned on its head, with a 6-4 record going into Week 12 well within the picture. That's heavily due to reinforcements arriving across the board, with Harrison and [Dell](https://fieldlevelmedia.com/news/texans-wr-tank-dell-knee-set-to-return-after-22-month-recovery/) for Stroud to throw to, Braden Smith up front, and [Henry To'oTo'o on his way back](https://www.yardbarker.com/nfl/articles/houston_texans_make_huge_announcement_on_wednesday/s1_17355_44427501) to a linebacker room that badly needs him. The offense has carried this team for two weeks. Get the defense back to even half of last year's Swarm, and the Texans go from "it's so over" to "we're so back."
